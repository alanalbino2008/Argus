# Data Processing Agreement

**Version {{version}}.** This agreement applies when an organisation (the **Customer**) uses Argus Business or Argus Professional and, through Argus, has **{{company}}** (the **Processor**) process personal data on its behalf. It forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It's designed to meet Article 28 of the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR.

## 1. What we process for you

- **Argus Connect lookups and AI analyst requests**: the details of a lookup, or the case material sent to the AI analyst, which may include personal data about people in the Customer's investigations. These pass through our service to the chosen source or AI provider, and the answer is returned. We don't store the content.
- **For Business teams**: the account details of team members the Customer adds (we hold these as described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy)).

Cases, evidence and notes stay on the Customer's computers and are not processed by us.

**Duration**: as long as the Customer's account is open. **Purpose**: providing Argus. **Data subjects**: people in the Customer's investigations; the Customer's team members. **Categories**: whatever the Customer chooses to look up or send, which may include identifiers, contact details, company roles and public online information.

## 2. Our commitments

We will:

1. process the data only on the Customer's documented instructions (using Argus as designed is such an instruction), unless the law requires otherwise, in which case we'll tell the Customer first unless the law forbids it;
2. ensure everyone authorised to process it is bound by confidentiality;
3. keep it secure with appropriate technical and organisational measures (encryption in transit and at rest, access control, logging, and keeping lookup content only in transit);
4. use only the subprocessors listed in [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors), under written terms that protect the data as well as this agreement. We'll give at least 30 days' notice of a new subprocessor, and the Customer may object; if we can't resolve the objection, the Customer may end the affected service and receive a pro-rata refund;
5. help the Customer answer requests from data subjects, and meet its obligations on security, breach notification, impact assessments and consultation with regulators, taking into account what we can do;
6. tell the Customer **without undue delay, and within 48 hours**, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Customer's data, with what we know and what we're doing about it;
7. when the service ends, delete the Customer's data (we hold no lookup or AI content; account data is handled as in the Privacy Policy), unless the law requires us to keep it;
8. make available the information needed to show we meet these commitments, and allow for and contribute to reasonable audits, on 30 days' notice, at the Customer's expense, at most once a year unless a breach or a regulator requires otherwise.

## 3. International transfers

Where data goes outside the UK or EEA, we'll use a lawful transfer mechanism, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, which are incorporated by reference.

## 4. The Customer's commitments

The Customer is the controller of its investigation data. It confirms it has a lawful basis for the processing it instructs, and follows the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).
