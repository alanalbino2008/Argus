# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version {{version}}.** Argus is a powerful investigation tool. This policy says what it may and may not be used for. It's part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It applies to everyone using Argus, including every member of a Business team.

## What Argus is for

Legitimate, proportionate investigations, for example:

- journalism and research in the public interest;
- due diligence on companies, suppliers, partners and investments;
- fraud, scam and financial crime investigations;
- security research, threat intelligence and assessing your own (or a client's authorised) exposure;
- legal work, including evidence for proceedings;
- safeguarding and protective work by people authorised to do it.

Argus asks for the purpose of each case and records it. Record it truthfully: it's how you, and we, can show the work was legitimate.

## What Argus must never be used for

- **Stalking, harassing, intimidating or threatening anyone**, or helping someone else to.
- **Watching a partner, ex-partner or family member** without a lawful basis.
- **Investigating or profiling children.** Argus is built to refuse this; don't try to get around it.
- **Publishing someone's private details to expose or harm them** ("doxxing").
- **Discrimination**: profiling people for their race, ethnicity, religion, sexuality, health, politics, trade union membership or any other protected characteristic, or to make decisions about them on that basis.
- **Getting into accounts, systems or data you're not authorised to access**, including using leaked passwords, or getting around logins, paywalls or other technical protections.
- **Scanning or testing systems** that you don't own and have no permission to test.
- **Impersonating people** or using false identities to get information, where that's unlawful.
- **Selling or trading personal information** you found with Argus, or building databases of people for that purpose.
- **Helping anyone avoid the law**, or any other unlawful purpose.

## Your obligations

- Collect only what you need for your purpose, and keep it only as long as you need it. Argus reminds you about cases you haven't touched in a long time.
- Keep what you collect secure. Argus encrypts what it stores, and offers a case vault and encrypted backups.
- Respect the terms of the sources you use, and the rights of the people your work is about.
- Make sure everyone in your team knows and follows this policy.

## Enforcement

We may investigate suspected misuse. That includes asking you about a case's recorded purpose. If we find misuse, we may warn you, suspend or close your account without refund, and, where the law requires or allows it, report it to the authorities.
