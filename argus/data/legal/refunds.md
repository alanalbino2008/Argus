# Refunds and Cancellation

**Version {{version}}.** This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## Cancelling a plan

You can cancel at any time: Settings → *Manage billing*. Your plan runs to the end of the period you've already paid for, then stops renewing. Your cases always stay on your computer, readable and exportable.

## Refunds for plans

- Payments for a period that has started aren't refunded, unless the law requires it or we close your account without a breach on your part. In that case we refund the unused part.
- If we change these terms or our prices and you don't accept the change, we refund any period paid in advance that hasn't started.
- If you've been charged in error, or charged for a renewal you'd already cancelled, tell us within 60 days and we'll refund it.

## Credit packs

- Credits you buy don't expire while your account is open.
- Unused credit packs can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Credits already used aren't refundable.
- When a payment for credits is refunded or disputed, the credits it bought are taken back.

## Consumers' right to cancel

If you're a consumer in the UK or EU, you have 14 days from purchase to cancel. When you start using a subscription or credits within those 14 days, you ask us to start straight away and accept that you lose the right to cancel for what you've used. We refund the rest.

## How to ask

Email {{contact}} from your account's email address, with the date and amount of the payment. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 working days.
