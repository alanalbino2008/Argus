# Privacy Policy

**Version {{version}}.** This policy explains what personal information **{{company}}**{{company_number_text}} ("we", "us"), {{address_text}}, holds about people who have an Argus account, why, and what rights they have. We are the **controller** of this information. Questions or requests: {{privacy_contact}}.

## The short version

- Argus Personal keeps your **cases on your own computer**. We never receive them.
- We hold what's needed to run your account: who you are, your plan and payments, your computers, and your credits.
- We don't sell your information, show you adverts, or track you around the web.
- You can download your account data, or delete your account, at any time in Settings.

## What we hold, and why

| What | Why | Lawful basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your email, name and password (stored only as a one-way hash) | To give you an account and let you sign in | Contract |
| Each computer signed in: a random identifier, the computer's name, when it was first and last seen | To enforce your plan's computer limit and let you sign computers out | Contract |

## What passes through, but isn't kept

- **Argus Connect lookups** (a company number, a name, a web search, an image address) go through our service to the source you chose, and the answer comes back to you. We keep only that a lookup of that kind was made, to count credits.
- **AI analyst requests**: the case material you choose to send passes through our service to our AI provider, and the answer comes back to you. We don't keep it. Our provider processes it under its business terms, which don't allow it to train on it.
- If you set your own API keys in Argus Personal, those requests go straight from your computer to the source and don't reach us at all.

## What we never have

Your cases, evidence, notes and scan results stay on your computer (and in your backups, which are encrypted with a password only you know). We can't see them, hand them over, or recover them.

## People in investigations

If you think you may be the subject of research done with Argus: the investigator, not us, holds and controls that information. They decided to collect it, and must respect your rights under data protection law. Please contact them. We can't see their cases, but if you believe Argus is being misused, tell us at {{contact}}: we take reports of misuse seriously ([Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use)).

## Who we share information with

Only the service providers we need, under contracts that protect it. The current list is in [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors). It includes our hosting provider, our email provider, Stripe for payments, and (only for the requests you send) our AI provider and paid data sources. We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

## International transfers

Some providers are outside the UK and EEA (for example in the United States). We transfer information to them only with the safeguards the law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or to countries with an adequacy decision.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account**: while it's open. When you delete it, your name, email, password, computers, emails and invitations are erased straight away, and the payment provider's record of you is deleted.
- **Payment records** are kept for 6 years after the end of the financial year they belong to, as tax law requires, attached to an anonymous account number.
- **Records of emails sent** are deleted after 6 months.
- **Security records** are kept for up to 6 years, to protect accounts and in case of legal claims. They are tamper-evident, so entries can't be quietly changed.

## Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information (or download it yourself: Settings → *Download my account data*), correct it, delete it (Settings → *Delete my account*), restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another service. We'll answer within one month. You can also complain to {{authority}}. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

## Cookies

We use only cookies that are strictly necessary: they keep you signed in and protect your session. No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

## Children

Argus is not for anyone under 18, and must never be used to investigate children.

## Security

Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes, connections are encrypted, secrets are encrypted at rest, and access to our systems is limited and logged. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

## Changes

We'll update this policy when what we do changes, and tell you about important changes before they apply.
