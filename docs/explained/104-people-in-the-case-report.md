# 104. People in the case report

The one-file case report (70, signed since 72) now has a **People researched** section, after the
summary, for each person researched in the case:

* **what was known**: names, usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, accounts, websites, town,
  employer, year of birth, and whether a photo was compared (the fingerprint itself is not shown);
* **the latest search**: when, by whom, how it ended, how many searches, and whether they were made
  directly or through a route (and passive-only in an anonymous case); whether the person is
  watched weekly;
* **what was confirmed as theirs**, each with its score, the reasons for it (and any against), who
  confirmed it, when, and their note.

How many candidates were **ruled out** and how many are **not yet reviewed** is given as a number
only. Ruled-out accounts are not listed: they belong to other people, who have nothing to do with the
case, and a report is handed to others.

The section is covered by the report's signature like everything else in it.
