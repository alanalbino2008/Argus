# 102. The People tab and the weekly watch

## The People tab

Each case has a **People** tab, next to Playbooks:

1. **Add person**: the name the case uses for them, and everything known, one item per line
   (names, usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, account links, websites), with town, employer
   and year of birth. Adding confirms the legitimate purpose and that the person is not a child.
2. **Photo** adds a known picture. Only its fingerprint is kept.
3. **Search from what is known**, after ticking the purpose: choose how far to follow what is found
   and whether to search everything again. Each search shows as it runs (where it came from, what
   it found, how many candidates were new).
4. **Candidates**, best first, each with its verdict and score, how it was found, and the reasons
   for and against. **Theirs** and **Not theirs** record the decision; the tabs show what is left to
   review, what is theirs, what is not, or everything.

Several people can be researched in one case; the tab shows how many candidates each has waiting.

## The weekly watch

**Watch weekly** searches again every 7 days from everything known (including what has been
confirmed since), so new accounts are found as they appear. When a watch finds new candidates
that are likely or possible, it raises **one alert**: "Known-details watch: 3 new candidates to
review (1 likely)".

The alert deliberately names neither the person nor the accounts: alerts can be sent on to chat and
email, and the details belong in the case. The alert carries the case's reference; the new candidates wait in its People tab.

* A watch counts towards the workspace's **watches** quota, with brand and fingerprint watches.
* A closed case turns its watches off; a refused search (for example, an anonymous case whose
  route is gone, or a quota reached) is shown as the watch's last result.
* Each watch is claimed before it runs, so with several replicas it runs once.
* The watch searches as "*name of whoever turned it on* (weekly watch)", audited like any search.
